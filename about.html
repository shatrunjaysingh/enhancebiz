About EnhanceBiz

We began by running the operations other companies depend on. Today we build IndicusAI, the agentic AI platform that puts that same discipline to work inside your systems, without taking custody of your data.

Our Story

Where operational discipline meets artificial intelligence

EnhanceBiz grew up in the business of getting things right for other people — customer support, back-office processing, and call-center operations delivered for clients around the world. That work taught us a simple truth: reliable outcomes come from disciplined process, relentless quality control, and people who genuinely care about the details.

As AI began reshaping every industry, we pointed that rigor first at the data that trains machine learning, building AnnotateMe, our annotation platform, and data services around it. Doing that work showed us what enterprises actually need next: AI that can do real operational work, and that a security team can say yes to.

That is IndicusAI: agents that read your systems in the moment, check their own conclusions, and show their evidence. It is now the focus of our company, built by people who know what it takes to run the operations these agents work on.
