Agents that do real work

Each solution is built on the same IndicusAI platform. Each one reads your systems, reaches a conclusion you can check, and knows when the right answer is to stop or hand over to a person.

Customer Assistant

Answers from your own systems, on every channel

Customers ask about a bill, order, booking or request. The assistant checks who is asking, fetches that one customer's record live from your system, and answers in their language, on your website or app, WhatsApp, SMS, email or a phone call.

  • Only the right customer. Signed in, their own number, a one-time code or a detail on the record. You choose how sure it must be for each question.
  • Only what you allow. You choose the fields it may see: the amount and due date, not the address or history.
  • Nothing copied. Answers are fetched live. We log that a lookup happened, not what it returned.

Live in about two weeks

Days 1–2: access to your API, your FAQ and your WhatsApp number.

Days 3–5: connect, set protections, test with test customers.

Days 6–10: pilot with your team.

Day 10 onward: live, with your team able to take over any conversation and hand it back.

Voice Agents

Outbound calling with the rules built in

Run announcement and conversational campaigns from a CSV or from an agent's own results, in Hindi, English and other Indian languages. Conversations follow a skill or question set you control, and every call feeds your reports.

  • Quiet hours as a ceiling. A campaign cannot widen them.
  • Do-not-call checks at dial time, not just at upload.
  • Per-number evidence export for every campaign.

Why it matters

Calling Indian consumers is regulated under TRAI rules. In IndicusAI, the rules that a user could otherwise override are enforced by the platform itself.

Smart Dispatch

From complaint to technician, without a dispatcher chasing phones

A customer reports a problem on WhatsApp or by phone. The system captures location and asset, checks warranty against your registry, and rings the nearest free technician. A rejection or silence moves to the next nearest automatically.

  • SLA watch. Technicians who are off course close to the deadline raise a red flag for supervisors.
  • The full loop. Live tracking and an OTP for the customer, a job page and app for the technician, plus billing, inventory and reports.
  • National coverage. Every Indian pincode known, with service partners, dealer counters and freelancers.

Configuration, not code

Built for any business that sends technicians to customers. A new client's hours, escalation rules, registry and WhatsApp number are settings.

Security Operations

Two alerts. One rule. Opposite conclusions.

The rule name tells you little. The context tells you everything. A four-agent pipeline triages each alert, enriches it with reputation, asset and identity data, then either contains the incident or tunes the detection that misfired.

  • Deliberately asymmetric. Anything not proven benign goes to containment.
  • Fixes the cause. A false positive ends with a proposed rule change, so it doesn't come back next week.

Demo scenario

Both alerts fire "Encoded PowerShell spawned by Office process" at high severity. One is Word on a finance user's laptop sending 8.8 MB to an external address. The other is a signed management agent sending 2.2 KB internally.

Demonstration on stand-in data.
Revenue Cycle

A denial is a deadline nobody is watching

Winning the appeal is rarely the hard part. Knowing which appeal is worth filing before the window closes is. The agent reads the claim and remittance, checks eligibility without being asked, and counts the clock.

  • A decision, not a score, with the evidence and the deadline.
  • It declines. When the payer is right, it says to bill the patient and explains why in plain words.

Demo scenario

A $1,240 crown denied for missing prior authorization. The agent finds a twelve-month waiting period that would sink the appeal anyway, recommends billing the patient, and flags 13 days left on the window. 38 seconds, 6.6 cents.

Demonstration on stand-in dental billing data.
Identity & Access Reviews

Certification cycles without the spreadsheet chase

Chasing owners, emailing reviewers, exporting and comparing lists: agents run the cycle end to end. Your people spend their time on the exceptions it flags rather than on assembling the information needed to find them.

Demo scenario

A full cycle completed in about five minutes for under a dollar, and surfaced seven categories of finding, including access that had been formally revoked and quietly restored.

Demonstration in a simulated environment.
