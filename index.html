Why IndicusAI

The agent is not the blocker. Data custody is.

Most agent platforms ask for your data first. That is where security review, residency questions and procurement slow everything down. So we built agents that visit your systems instead of warehousing them.

The usual agent platform

  • Copies your records into the vendor's platform
  • Adds a new trust boundary for security to review
  • Answers from a sync that may already be out of date
  • Retention is a line in a policy document

IndicusAI

  • Reads the source system only when it needs an answer
  • Your credentials stay the authorization boundary
  • Your systems stay the system of record
  • Retention is a setting, and our test suite checks it by reading the database back
How It Works

Reach your systems through MCP or your existing APIs, scoped tool by tool. A connector covers systems behind your firewall with no inbound port opened.

Security & Control

Built for the security review

Metadata-only retention

No prompts or tool results stored. Cost, latency and outcome still are, so you can run the thing.

Your cloud, your models

Run hosted or in your VPC, and route model calls through Amazon Bedrock or Google Vertex.

Access you can audit

Role-based access, SSO and SCIM, with deprovisioning that actually removes access.

People stay in charge
